Account & Data Deletion

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and global privacy regulations, users can permanently delete their Organizo account and associated personal data at any time.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the Organizo mobile app installed on your device, you can delete your account instantly:

  • 1
    Open the Organizo app on your mobile device.
  • 2
    Tap the Profile icon in the top header or navigation.
  • 3
    Scroll to the bottom and tap the red Delete Account button.
  • 4
    Confirm the prompt. Your personal data is scrubbed instantly and your session is closed.
