Account & Data Deletion

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and global privacy regulations, users can permanently delete their Organizo account and associated personal data at any time.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the Organizo mobile app installed on your device, you can delete your account instantly:

  • 1
    Open the Organizo app on your mobile device.
  • 2
    Tap the Profile icon in the top header or navigation.
  • 3
    Scroll to the bottom and tap the red Delete Account button.
  • 4
    Confirm the prompt. Your personal data is scrubbed instantly and your session is closed.

Method 2: Instant Web Deletion with OTP Verification

If you uninstalled the mobile app, you can verify your identity via OTP and permanently delete your account right here:

Verification Code Sent: We sent a 6-digit OTP to your registered email address. Please enter it below to confirm permanent deletion.

✓ Account Permanently Deleted

Your Organizo account and all associated personal data have been scrubbed from our systems. Any past group ledger entries have been safely anonymized as "Former Member".

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I recover my account once deleted?

No. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed. If you want to use Organizo again in the future, you will need to sign up as a new user.

What if I am an organizer of active events?

Before deleting your account, ensure all pending participant claims are approved or rejected, and settle any outstanding debts to avoid leaving incomplete ledger states for other members.
