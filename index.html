Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Is Organizo free to use?
Yes! Organizo is completely free for managing trips, events, and splitting group expenses with your friends.
How does expense splitting work?
Whenever you or a friend pays for a shared bill (e.g. hotel room or dinner), enter the amount and select who was included. Organizo automatically updates everyone's net balance.
Can I use Organizo without internet?
Absolutely! You can record expenses offline. Once your device gets an internet connection, your changes will automatically sync.
