Privacy Policy

Your privacy and the security of your group financial data are fundamental to us.

Last Updated & Effective: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Organizo ("we," "our," or "us"). Organizo provides mobile applications and web services designed to assist users with organizing group trips, events, coordinating shared expenses, tracking collections, and computing optimal peer-to-peer repayments.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you access or use the Organizo mobile app and associated services at organizo.co.in. By using Organizo, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, as well as data gathered automatically when using the app:

  • Account & Profile Information: Full Name, Email Address, and Phone Number provided during registration or profile setup.
  • Trip & Event Financial Data: Event names, trip itineraries, budgets, expense items, amounts, split allocations, contributor names, and settlement records logged within groups.
  • User-Generated Media: Expense receipts, event banners, and organizer UPI payment QR codes uploaded by users.
  • Device & Usage Data: Device model, operating system version, push notification identifiers (FCM / OneSignal Player ID), and basic diagnostic logs for troubleshooting sync issues.

3. Device Permissions & Why We Need Them

The Organizo Android application may request specific device permissions strictly to enable core features:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used to take real-time photos of paper receipts or capture UPI QR codes for event collections.
  • Photos & Media (android.permission.READ_MEDIA_IMAGES): Used to attach existing receipt screenshots and UPI payment QR codes from your gallery.
  • Push Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to send real-time alerts when group expenses are logged, settlements are cleared, or event organizers broadcast announcements.
  • Internet & Network State: Used to communicate with our secure backend APIs and detect offline mode for seamless outbox queue syncing.

Note: We never access your camera, media, or files without your explicit permission and action.

4. Third-Party Service Providers & Data Disclosures

We work with trusted third-party service providers to power key infrastructure:

  • Razorpay: Handles secure payment processing for digital pass upgrades. Financial billing details are handled directly by Razorpay in compliance with PCI-DSS standards.
  • OneSignal & Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Manages encrypted push notification dispatch and device registration.
  • Cloudinary: Provides secure cloud storage for uploaded receipt attachments and payment QR images.
  • Email Services (Nodemailer / Brevo / SMTP): Dispatches 6-digit verification OTP codes for secure passwordless sign-in and email updates.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

5. Data Security & Storage

All data transmitted between your device and Organizo servers is encrypted in transit using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS) protocols. Sensitive tokens and credentials are securely stored using token versioning and salted hashes.

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active to provide ongoing group history, expense records, and settlement summaries.

6. Account & Data Deletion (Your Rights)

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and global privacy regulations (GDPR, DPDP Act 2023), you have the absolute right to delete your account and request data erasure at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Go to My Profile → Delete Account inside the Organizo app to permanently initiate account deletion.
  • Web-Based Deletion Request: You can submit a deletion request directly on our dedicated web page at organizo.co.in/delete-account.html without needing to reinstall the app.

Ledger Anonymization Notice: Upon deletion, your personal identity (name, email, phone, push tokens) is permanently wiped. Any past shared split expenses or contributions in group trips are anonymized as "Former Member" so that your friends' historical calculations and debts remain mathematically accurate.
